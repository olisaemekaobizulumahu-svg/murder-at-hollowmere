The Hollowmere case file · 11:47 pm

The night the clock stopped

Great-Uncle Ambrose invited his family to the manor with a promise: the new will would settle everything. It did the opposite. A door slammed as the storm reached the lake. A servant heard footsteps above the music room. At 11:47, the grandfather clock stopped. When the guests gathered, Ambrose was dead.

Six relatives had reasons to dread his next announcement. Six strange methods have been whispered about. Six rooms remain under suspicion. No witness knows the full truth—and every new game hides a different answer.

Atmospheric details set the scene; only your cards, questions and confirmed deductions establish the solution.
